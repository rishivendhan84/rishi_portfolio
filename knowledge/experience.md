# Experience

## Different Hair Pvt. Ltd & CX Analytix: Full Stack Developer (May 2025 to present, Chennai)

Rishi builds production web apps and GenAI systems end to end. Details of internal systems, data and clients are not public; the items below describe what he built and the technology, not company internals.

## Production Planning System (Different Hair)

An 8-module production planning platform that digitized a fully manual, handwritten process. It tracks work orders, SKUs, bills of resources (BORs) and production stations, with a real-time timeline that follows each work order through its full lifecycle and role-based access control. Rishi architected it and owned it from design through deployment, working with stakeholders and company leadership. Stack: React, Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL. The system manages 9,000+ work orders, 4,500+ SKUs, 16 BORs and 8 stations. It was delivered through phase-one stakeholder user acceptance testing (UAT).

## Production RAG Chatbot (Different Hair)

A RAG chatbot over complex internal data, built with vector database retrieval and LangChain in Python, and deployed as a live embeddable chat widget that gives users self-serve answers to repetitive support questions. A public demo of the widget: https://hc-chat-widget.vercel.app

## Multi-Platform Content Agent

An autonomous multi-agent system that produces complete social posts (images, copy, captions and hashtags) from a single prompt, tailored separately for 8 platforms: Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, Pinterest, X and a blog. Stack: LangChain, LangGraph, n8n.

## SEO Analytics Agent

An agent that ingests Google Search Console and GA4 data and delivers scheduled weekly SEO, AEO and GEO reports automatically. Stack: Python, n8n, GA4, Search Console.

## Email-Marketing Analytics (CX Analytix, B2B)

A B2B analytics product that surfaces email campaign KPIs through live dashboards, giving clients real-time visibility into performance. Stack: Node.js, Python, PostgreSQL.

## Timesheet Automation

A human-in-the-loop RPA workflow that tracks activity and pre-fills ClickUp timesheets for review before submission. It has been tested personally; it is not presented as a team-wide rollout.
