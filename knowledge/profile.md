# Rishi Vendhan K K: Profile

## Summary

Rishi Vendhan K K is a Full Stack Engineer based in Chennai, India, with about 4.5 years of experience shipping production web applications end to end, from architecture and API design through deployment. He works across React.js, Node.js / Express, Python and SQL / NoSQL databases (PostgreSQL, MongoDB, MSSQL), and builds GenAI systems: RAG, multi-agent workflows and knowledge graphs.

He currently works as a Full Stack Developer at Different Hair Pvt. Ltd & CX Analytix (since May 2025). Before that he was a Software Engineer at Minsway Solutions Pvt Ltd from December 2021 to May 2025.

## How he works

Rishi owns features end to end and works closely with stakeholders and leadership in fast-paced teams. His focus is turning manual, expensive workflows into software that real users depend on. In AI work he favors patterns where the model handles fuzzy language and deterministic code handles state and numbers, for example a validator that checks every AI-proposed change before it is committed.
